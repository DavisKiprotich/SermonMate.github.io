Data Deletion

Last updated: October 15, 2026

SermonMate does not require or provide a SermonMate user account. There is therefore no account profile or account database for us to delete.

Delete data stored on your device

To remove local SermonMate data, use one of these options:

  1. In your device settings, open Apps, select SermonMate, then choose Storage and Clear data; or
  2. Uninstall SermonMate from your device.

Either option removes locally stored settings, bookmarks, reading progress, downloaded Bible/story/audio content, chat display history, and local access tokens from that device.

Subscriptions and purchases

Removing the app or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play or the Apple App Store. Those stores and RevenueCat maintain subscription and billing records under their own policies.

AI-chat and cloud requests

SermonMate does not create a personal account record for AI chat. AI requests are processed to generate a response and may be handled by Cloudflare, OpenAI, Google AI, and, in limited legacy configurations, Supabase. These providers may retain technical or security logs in accordance with their own policies.

Need help?

If you need help clearing data or have a privacy question, email devmile45@gmail.com. Include your device platform and app version, but do not include sensitive AI-chat content, payment information, or passwords.
