Before contacting us

Please include your device platform (Android or iPhone), app version, what you expected to happen, and any error message. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, or sensitive AI-chat content.

Common help topics

  • Subscription or restore purchase: Confirm that you are signed in to the same Google Play or Apple App Store account used for the purchase, then use the app's Restore Purchases option.
  • Subscription cancellation or refund: Manage the subscription or contact the store through which you purchased. SermonMate cannot access your payment-card information.
  • Story, image, audio, or Bible download issue: Check your internet connection, reopen the app, and try again. Downloaded content is retained locally for offline use where available.
  • AI chat unavailable: Confirm your premium access is active and that the device has an internet connection. AI chat is cloud-based and may be unavailable temporarily. If the app says your access is required, restore your purchase or check your subscription in the relevant app store.
  • Reset app data / start again: Open Library > Settings > Reset app data, then confirm. This removes local downloads, saved items, bookmarks, chat history, settings, and reviewer access from that device and starts onboarding again. It does not cancel a subscription.
  • Delete app data: See the Data Deletion page.
Safety notice: SermonMate AI chat is for Bible-based encouragement, not medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or emergency advice. If you are in immediate danger or crisis, contact local emergency services or a trusted qualified professional.
