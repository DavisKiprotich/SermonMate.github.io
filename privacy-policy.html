Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

SermonMate ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • App Preferences: Language, theme (dark/light mode), and settings
  • Bookmarks: Saved Bible verses and stories
  • Chat History: Recent AI chat conversation data stored locally on your device for the in-app experience
  • Reading Progress: Stories you've read

Preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, and locally stored chat display data are stored on your device. Messages you send to the AI chat feature are transmitted as needed to generate AI responses.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version (for app compatibility)
  • Subscription Status: Processed securely through Apple App Store or Google Play Store
  • AI Chat Prompts: Messages you send to the AI chat feature are sent through our Supabase Edge Function to our AI provider so the app can generate a response

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Bible study features and personalized content
  • Power AI-driven chat responses for spiritual guidance
  • Process subscriptions through App Store/Google Play
  • Improve app performance and user experience

AI-Powered Features

SermonMate uses artificial intelligence (AI) to provide personalized Bible chat responses. When you use AI chat, your message is sent through our Supabase Edge Function to our AI provider to generate relevant scripture-based guidance. Chat display history is stored locally on your device.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your payment information directly. All payment data is handled securely by Apple or Google.

Data Storage and Security

  • Bookmarks, preferences, reading progress, and chat display history are stored locally on your device
  • AI chat messages are transmitted only as needed to provide AI-powered responses
  • We do not maintain user accounts or login systems
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Supabase: Secure backend routing for AI chat requests
  • Google AI: Powers the AI chat feature
  • Apple/Google: Subscription payment processing

Your Rights

  • Data Control: You control locally stored preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, and chat display history on your device
  • Deletion: Uninstall the app to delete all data
  • No Account: No account creation required to use the app

Children's Privacy

SermonMate is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
