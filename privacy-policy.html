Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

SermonMate ("we", "our", or "us") is a Bible-study mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SermonMate.

Information stored on your device

SermonMate does not require you to create a SermonMate account. The app stores the following on your device to provide its features:

  • app preferences, including language, theme, and settings;
  • bookmarks and reading progress;
  • downloaded Bible content, story content, images, and audio for offline use;
  • recent AI-chat display history; and
  • an anonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier and, where applicable, a time-limited reviewer-access token used to verify access.

Information sent when you use online features

Stories, media, and Bible downloads

Stories, images, audio, and Bible-download files are delivered from Cloudflare R2 and Cloudflare's content-delivery network. When your device requests this content, Cloudflare may process standard technical request information, such as IP address, device/browser information, and request logs, under its own privacy practices.

AI chat

When you use AI chat, the message you submit, the recent conversation context needed to respond, your selected language, platform, and anonymous subscription identifier are sent to our Cloudflare Worker. The Worker verifies premium access through RevenueCat and sends the request to an AI provider, currently OpenAI or Google AI (Gemini), to generate a response. A legacy Supabase route may be used only if the Cloudflare AI-chat route is unavailable in an older app configuration.

We do not intentionally use AI-chat prompts to build a personal profile or store them as a SermonMate account record. Our infrastructure and AI providers may process limited technical and security logs in accordance with their own policies. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, financial, legal, confidential, or crisis information in AI chat.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and subscription-entitlement information so the app can verify premium access.

How we use information

  • to provide and remember app features on your device;
  • to deliver cloud-hosted stories, audio, images, and Bible downloads;
  • to provide AI-powered Bible and spiritual-guidance responses;
  • to verify subscriptions and prevent abuse of premium or reviewer access; and
  • to maintain the reliability and security of the app.

Service providers

Depending on the feature used, SermonMate uses Cloudflare (Worker and R2 delivery), RevenueCat (subscription management and entitlement verification), OpenAI and Google AI (AI responses), Supabase (legacy backend compatibility), Google Play, and Apple App Store (payment processing). These providers handle information under their respective policies.

Your choices and deletion

Because SermonMate does not require a user account, there is no account profile for us to delete. You can use Library > Settings > Reset app data in SermonMate to remove local downloads, saved stories, bookmarks, chat display history, settings, reviewer access, and local Bible content from that device, then start onboarding again. You can also clear app data in your device settings or uninstall the app. Resetting, clearing data, or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription; purchases, refunds, and billing records must be managed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. See our Data Deletion page for details.

Children's privacy

SermonMate is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy here and change the "Last updated" date.
